### Fixed
- **Sky-Watcher direct driver: a garbled ack no longer makes a started move report as rejected** (AlpacaCore, issue #988; `skywatcher_protocol_wrapper.cpp`): when the board applied a `:J` start but its ack arrived malformed or mis-paired, the one resend drew `!2` (motor not stopped) and surfaced `MotorControllerRejected` for an axis that was running. A `!2` answer to the resent `:J` is now taken as the start having landed; a first-frame `!` still throws, inquiries keep their one resend and stops keep their retransmit. Two cases in `test_skywatcher_serial.cpp` pin both sides.
