### Fixed
- **SynScan: southern-hemisphere tracking no longer switches the handset into PEC mode** (AlpacaCore, found investigating issue #1030): `Tracking=true` at a southern site sent `T3`, which the Sky-Watcher SynScan Serial Communication Protocol v3.3 defines as PEC mode (Sidereal + PEC), not "EQ south" (that is Celestron's numbering); equatorial tracking now sends `T2` in either hemisphere and the handset applies its own site, so a handset holding a PEC recording no longer plays it back during every tracked session south of the equator.
- **SynScan: GOTO landings read the handset's RA at its once-a-second tick, so ConformU's own read agrees** (AlpacaCore, issue #1030): the handset advances its local sidereal time once a second, so after a GOTO its RA readback is a sawtooth, exact right after the tick and up to 15" low just before the next; the landing refinement read it at one phase and a client at another, which is the "10.4 arc seconds away from RA target" ConformU reported (raw capture on an EQM-35 Pro, HC 06.03.00: 194 counts, 1.00 s period, steady for over two minutes after every GOTO). After each GOTO the driver now reads until it sees the jump, anchors the tick, takes the landing residual from the read right after it, and corrects every `RightAscension` read for the following two minutes by the fraction of the handset's second elapsed since the tick; a tracking-mode or time write and connect/disconnect drop the anchor, and a handset without the sawtooth is read as before after a 1.3 s search.
