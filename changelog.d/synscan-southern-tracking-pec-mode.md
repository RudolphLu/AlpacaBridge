### Fixed
- **SynScan: southern-hemisphere tracking no longer switches the handset into PEC mode** (AlpacaCore, found investigating issue #1030): `Tracking=true` at a southern site sent `T3`, which the Sky-Watcher SynScan Serial Communication Protocol v3.3 defines as PEC mode (Sidereal + PEC), not "EQ south" (that is Celestron's numbering); equatorial tracking now sends `T2` in either hemisphere and the handset applies its own site, so a handset holding a PEC recording no longer plays it back during every tracked session south of the equator.
- **SynScan: GOTO landings are read at the handset's once-a-second tick and finished with timed moves instead of more GOTOs** (AlpacaCore, issue #1030): the handset advances its local sidereal time once a second, so after a GOTO its RA readback is a sawtooth, exact right after the tick and up to 15" low just before the next; the landing refinement read it at one phase and a client at another, which is the "10.4 arc seconds away from RA target" ConformU reported. The handset also re-rolls every GOTO landing by a whole second of RA and lands short Dec moves up to 19" off, so the re-GOTO refinement could not converge and added reversals after every slew. After each GOTO the driver now finds the tick (three jumps, about 30 ms), takes the landing from the post-tick read, finishes the residual with guide-rate moves on each axis (0.3" in Dec, a few arcseconds in RA on the EQM-35 Pro), and corrects every `RightAscension` read for the following two minutes by the elapsed fraction of the handset's second, picking the side of the tick from the last known RA. An explicit tracking write, a time write and connect/disconnect drop the anchor; a handset without the sawtooth is read as before after a 1.3 s search.
